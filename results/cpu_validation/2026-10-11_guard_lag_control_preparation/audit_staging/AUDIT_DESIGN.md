## Independent planning mathematics

`lag_control_audit_math.py` pins and imports the unchanged stdlib-only original
`scripts/audit_guard_lag_offline.py` (bede59d...); it never imports online helpers
or loaders. Exactly the existing H15, 81 product-order constant vectors,
per-axis .5 permission, original costs, bounds, velocity1.5 forecast bound,
exact tie order and zero fallback are retained.

Every candidate reinitializes q/v from the same current native observation and
previous_v from the preceding returned native velocity endpoint. At each
candidate step, start-G uses its own q; `previous_v = old current predicted v`
after prediction. No constant initial history, candidate-to-candidate carry,
future observed velocity or real/applied torque enters a rollout. Current
target only is visible, and the prior actual requested command supplies the
unchanged first-step change cost.

Rejected endpoints remain paid pair/scalar predictions. They are excluded from
approved prediction trajectories and cost, matching the original controller's
step order. Nonfinite head failures retain explicit pair/scalar attempts and
successful head counts. An unknown online failure must remain a lower bound
and cannot be silently reconstructed as a successful planner call.
